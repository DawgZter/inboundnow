## Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the Remote for Startups partner program?

We partner with various regional and global entities, such as incubators, accelerators, venture capital and private equity firms, angel syndicates, and other startup-enabling organisations who actively invest in and support the growth of startups. We're looking for a mutually beneficial relationship where partners promote Remote's benefits within their community.

Why is Remote offering a discount for startups?

We understand cash is an important resource at this stage in your growth as a startup. With that in mind, we're offering promotional pricing for qualified startups that would benefit from using Remote as a tool to fuel their growth.

What are the key benefits to joining this program?

By joining the Remote for Startups program as a partner, you'll be able to empower your portfolio companies with one easy solution to legally and compliantly employ global talent.

Additionally, Startups referred through the Remote for Startups program can enjoy exclusive pricing, personalised educational materials and resources, as well as access to extra benefits, such as access to other perks listed in our Partner Marketplace .

How do I join the program?

Fill out our interest form here, and we'll get back to you shortly.

What if I'm a startup myself? How do I take advantage of the program?

If you're a startup yourself - we've got you covered! As long as you fit our criteria, you can take advantage of special startup pricing, which consists of a 15% discount on Remote’s services. You can learn more here .
However, if your investor is a Remote for Startups partner, you may be eligible for an even greater discount. Not sure if you qualify? Chat with us.

My investor is not part of the Remote for Startups program. How can I still take advantage?

If your investor is not a Remote for Startups partner, we'd love to chat with them! Introduce us, and you'll get an additional 5% off (up to a 20% discount).
