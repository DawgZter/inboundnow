# Your checklist for managing and paying international contractors

## Considering working with contractors?

Managing contractors doesn’t have to be complex or challenging. Here are a few things to keep in mind while choosing a contractor management solution.

### Locally compliant contractor agreements

- Managing contractors doesn’t have to be complex or challenging. Here are a few things to keep in mind while choosing a contractor management solution.

- Look for a system that offers country specific contract templates that are compliant with local tax and regulatory laws.

- Make sure to use a separate statement of work that outlines contractor roles and responsibilities.

- Look for a system that supports assigning and managing employer signatories to contractor agreements.

- Ensure that system offers has optionality to opt for indemnity coverage against misclassification risks.

- Make sure to opt for a system that offers a KYB/KYC process to ensure compliance.

### Automated onboarding

- Look for self-serve onboarding to ensure seamless initiation for your contractor talent.

- Consider opting for an automated identity verification to streamline processes and minimize human error.

- Ensure the system can handle essential documentation and compliance checks.

### Invoices, Timesheets and Payments

- Look for a system that supports manual and automated invoice management for both individual and bulk invoices.

- Ensure if the system enables employers to set up invoices on behalf of the contractor. This is particularly useful when you’re managing huge number of contractors.

- Make sure invoice approval permissions can be owned and shared within your administrative team.

- Consider opting for a system that supports invoicing in specific time intervals, for example: weekly, biweekly, semi monthly, monthly.

- Consider opting for a system that supports invoicing and receiving payouts in a desired currency of choice.

- Prioritize guaranteed payouts for contractors. Opt for a system that removes the hassle of FX and currency conversion with controls in place to pay out contractors their exact invoice amount.

- Understand if you’re allowed to export invoices for specific time periods.

### Platform, user experience and pricing

- Consider opting for a system that offers a trial rollout of their service prior to paying their fee.

- Prioritize reducing time and effort by opting for a system that has higher automations.

- Look for a platform that will grow with you as your business grows. Consider knowing how many contractors use the platform on an average to know the platform scalability.

- Opt for multi-product suites over individual systems for prioritized efficiency. These suites offer flexible and scalable HR solutions, ideal for effectively managing your globally distributed team, including contractors.

- Base your decisions on platform usability. Ensure the platform is user-friendly and easily accessible for both you and your contractors.

- Prioritize having access to a highly knowledgeable and dedicated customer support team.

- Look for a platform that offers activity based pricing. Remote’s transparent pricing guarantee ensures you are charged only per active contractor per month.

### Understand your total cost of deployment

- Double check to ensure if the system is adding a ‘management fee’ on top of the monthly subscription.

- Understand if there is any additional costs deducted by the system for every invoice processed.

- Factor in the FX rate and bank transfer fees while setting up international contractor payments.
