### Understanding Barbados' labor laws

Barbados has specific labor laws that govern the engagement of contractors, and businesses must ensure they are aware of these regulations to avoid legal issues. It is important to correctly classify workers to avoid potential fines or penalties for misclassification. For example, contractors are considered independent workers and do not typically enjoy the same benefits as employees, such as paid leave or health benefits. However, it is essential to provide fair compensation and comply with the terms agreed upon in the contract.
