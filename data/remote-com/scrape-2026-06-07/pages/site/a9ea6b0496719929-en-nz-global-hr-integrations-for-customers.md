## Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I use an integration with Remote?

Our integrations connect the HR, accounting, and payroll tools you already use with Remote, so you can streamline hiring, onboarding, and people management processes. All of our integrations are designed to help you save time and reduce manual data entry across multiple platforms to more easily share and act on information across your tech stack.

How do I install the integrations?

Log in to your Remote account. Then navigate to the Integrations tab from the left menu. Select the integration you wish to install and follow the on-screen instructions to activate the integration. Please note, some integrations may require you to start or complete the installation on the partner's platform. In those cases, you will need to log in to your account with the specific partner to learn more and ensure you have the correct permissions to start or complete the installation in their platform.

Sounds great, but what do the integrations cost?

All of our integrations are free for our customers, and you can install as many integrations as you like. Please keep in mind that some of our partners require payment or a subscription to use their product before you can integrate it with Remote.

How do I become an integration partner?

We believe we’re better together. That’s why we’re always on the lookout for partners who want to connect their people, payroll, and other systems to Remote through software integrations and our Remote API. Visit this page to reach out to us and chat more about your options.

I am a Remote customer, where can I find your developer resources for the API to build a bespoke integration for my team?

Our Remote API documentation has everything you need to easily consolidate and share global employee data across platforms.

Is it possible to integrate Remote with some tools I use daily, like Slack, Asana and others?

Yes, it is possible. We are now integrated with Zapier. With over 5,000 apps supported by Zapier, you can now seamlessly connect Remote with your preferred tools, creating Zaps to automate workflows without complex configuration. Visit our page in Zapier here to see what is possible.

What if I use a tool that isn’t integrated with Remote or Zapier?

We’re always open to new uses that will improve your day-to-day. You can submit your integration or API requests using this form and we will review them with our Product team to consider adding it to our roadmap.
