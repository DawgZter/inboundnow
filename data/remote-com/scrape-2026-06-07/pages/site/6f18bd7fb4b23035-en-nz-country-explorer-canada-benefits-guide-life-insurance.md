## Life Insurance

Remote offers comprehensive Life Insurance and Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) benefits in Canada, aligned with the health insurance tier you select. This means that the Life Insurance and AD&D coverage levels are directly tied to the health insurance plan you choose.
The Basic tier includes a flat $25,000 coverage for both Life Insurance and AD&D, while higher tiers offer coverage based on a multiple of the employee’s annual earnings, with additional coverage for spouses and dependent children. The Premium Enhanced tier provides the highest level of coverage, up to 3x annual earnings, ensuring robust protection for employees and their families.
