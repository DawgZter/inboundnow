# What is a performance bonus?

## What is a performance bonus?

A performance bonus is a financial reward given to employees as recognition for achieving specific goals, or for exceptional performance. It is a variable component of pay that supplements your employee’s regular pay, and is typically tied to specific targets or metrics.
Performance bonuses can have a positive impact in several ways, such as:

- Motivation. They encourage employees to perform at their best by linking rewards to performance.

- Recognition. They acknowledge and reward employees for their contributions and achievements.

- Retention. They help retain top talent by creating more competitive compensation packages.

- Alignment. They align your employees' goals with your company’s strategic objectives, driving overall business success.

## Examples of a performance bonus

Performance bonuses can come in many shapes and sizes, but typically, they are one of (or a variation of) the following:

- Individual performance bonus. A salesperson exceeding their quarterly sales target might receive a performance bonus of 10% of their base pay.

- Team performance bonus. A project team that completes a critical project ahead of schedule and under budget might receive a shared bonus, divided equally among team members.

- Company performance bonus. Employees might receive a bonus if the company achieves its annual profit targets, with the bonus amount based on a percentage of their annual pay.

- Customer satisfaction bonus. Customer service representatives might receive bonuses based on achieving high customer satisfaction ratings or resolving a certain number of customer issues efficiently.

## Benefits of offering performance bonuses

While the rewards are obvious for employees, performance bonuses can also benefit you as the employer, too. For instance, they can:

- Enhance performance. As the name suggests, performance bonuses motivate employees to achieve high performance and exceed their targets. This leads to improved productivity and business outcomes.

- Improve employee satisfaction. Recognising and rewarding employees' efforts through performance bonuses can enhance job satisfaction and morale.

- Boost retention and attraction. By their nature, performance bonuses help you attract and retain top talent.

- Drive alignment and success. Tying bonuses to specific performance metrics ensures that your employees focus on key business objectives, and contribute to your company’s success.

## Best practices for performance bonuses

If you’re going to offer performance bonuses, ensure you:

- Define the criteria. Create clear and measurable performance criteria for awarding the bonuses.

- Conduct regular reviews. Conduct regular performance reviews to assess the effectiveness of your rewards program, and to ensure fairness.

- Communicate the program clearly. Clearly communicate the bonus structure and criteria to employees, so that they understand how their performance impacts their compensation.

- Budget accordingly. Don’t promise people something you can’t afford. Thoroughly plan and budget for performance bonuses to make sure they’re deliverable and sustainable.
