# Complexities in Global Hiring - Winning Strategies: How top businesses master global hiring and compliance

Discover global hiring trends and challenges with guest speakers from Deloitte Tax, Bytez, and Remote. Gain expert perspectives on international employment laws, recruiting strategies, and managing payroll across multiple countries.

## Are you equipped to navigate recruitment in today’s global landscape?

Featuring Adam Scheinman, Strategy & Transformation Senior Manager at Deloitte Tax, and Holly Peck, Co-founder and Head of Product at Bytez, this session delves into the nuances of international recruitment. From trends shaping the employment landscape to top challenges facing organizations today, explore a range of topics including legal considerations, compliance, and effective recruitment strategies.

Get firsthand insights from industry experts on navigating diverse regulatory environments, sourcing top talent, and managing payroll across borders. Whether you're an HR professional, business owner, or aspiring global entrepreneur, this webinar equips you with the knowledge and tools to thrive in the ever-evolving world of international talent acquisition. Don't miss out on this invaluable opportunity to expand your global hiring toolkit.
