## FAQs: Global Mobility, Relocation, and Immigration

What does transparent pricing for relocation and mobility mean?

We give you a full breakdown of service costs, timelines, and requirements before you submit a request. No hidden fees.

Do I need my own legal entity to use Remote Mobility?

Only if the employee is not hired through our EOR service. If needed, we can help you set up an entity.

How long does a relocation or mobility case take?

It depends on the country and service type. In many cases employees can be ready to work in under 10 business days from submission.

Can Remote Mobility help with existing employees, not just new hires?

Yes. We support pre-hire checks, active employee moves, remote work compliance, and business travel assessments.

What if you cannot support my relocation or immigration request?

You will receive an honest assessment, and if we cannot assist you we will let you know in the outcome of our assessment. No fee will be charged if we cannot assist.

Which types of immigration services are included?

Pre-hire assessments, immigration guidance, post hire employment changes and updates, citizenship support and assistance with family members.
