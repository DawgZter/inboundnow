# What is a cross-functional team?

A cross-functional team is a group of individuals from different functional areas or departments within an organisation who collaborate to achieve a common goal or complete a specific project. These teams usually consist of members with diverse skills, expertise, and perspectives, bringing together a range of talents to address complex challenges and tasks. The primary goal of a cross-functional team is to enhance communication, foster innovation, and take a holistic approach to problem-solving by leveraging its members' varied knowledge and experiences.
For example, Talon.One created a cross-functional team to research and decide on an Employer of Record (EOR) provider. Another company, Checkly , uses cross-functional DevOps teams to monitor and test software continually.
Utilising cross-functional teams can yield several benefits for organisations that strive to enhance efficiency, innovation, and overall performance. The individuals who compose these teams have diverse skills and expertise from different functional areas. They bring a variety of perspectives and strengths to the table, creating a collaborative environment that goes beyond the siloed structure of traditional departmental setups.

### A wide range of perspectives

These teams can tap into a broad spectrum of knowledge and experiences. When team members come from various departments, each person brings a unique skill set and a different collection of insights from their respective roles. This diversity gives a well-rounded approach to solving problems and making decisions.
For instance, a project involving product development may involve a cross-functional team of individuals from marketing, engineering, and finance, as their combined expertise can lead to a more comprehensive and balanced solution.
This kind of collaboration can expose team members to tools, processes, or methods that other departments frequently use. Team members can then take these strategies back to their departments, which can improve productivity, efficiency, and communication.

### Improved communication

Collaboration within cross-functional teams facilitates improved communication across the organisation. Because team members represent different departments, they become conduits through which information flows, breaking down communication barriers that often exist between these units.
This not only enhances internal communication but also promotes a better understanding of the broader organisational objectives. As team members work together toward a common goal, they naturally share information, ideas, and feedback. This leads to a more transparent and cohesive work environment.

### A culture of collaboration

Cross-functional team members must learn to work together to make decisions, solve problems, and reach the organisation's goals. This can foster a culture of collaboration and togetherness within the company.

### Agility and adaptability

In rapidly changing business environments, the ability to respond quickly to new challenges and opportunities is essential. By their nature, cross-functional teams are more flexible and adaptable than traditional, functionally oriented teams.
The diverse expertise within a cross-functional team allows for a faster response to changes in the market, technology, or organisational priorities. Such adaptability is particularly valuable in industries where innovation and quick decision-making are necessary to stay competitive.

### More innovation

The combination of different perspectives and skills in a cross-functional team creates a fertile ground for creativity and innovation. Team members expose each other to ideas and approaches that may not have come up within the confines of their individual departments. Collaboration by diverse minds often leads to innovative solutions and novel approaches to challenges. Plus, thinking outside the box is increasingly important in today's business landscape.

### Better employee engagement and satisfaction

Working in a team that values and leverages each member's expertise fosters a sense of ownership and accomplishment. Employees feel empowered when team members recognise their contributions and integrate them into the team's collective efforts. This can lead to higher job satisfaction, improved morale, and a greater sense of purpose among team members.
In addition, being exposed to different aspects of the organisation can broaden individual employees' skill sets, which can contribute to professional development and career growth.

### Effective problem-solving

When complex challenges arise, the combined knowledge and skills within a cross-functional team can offer a holistic approach to finding solutions. The diversity of thought inherent in this type of team helps identify potential blind spots and guarantees a thorough examination of issues from multiple angles.

### Stronger work relationships

Cross-functional teams allow employees to work with people outside of their own departments, meet and get to know other people, and collaborate with others. Developing strong workplace bonds between employees can help reduce employee turnover and improve the organisation's overall health.

### Improved conflict resolution skills

Within any team composed of members with differing work styles, backgrounds, and experiences, conflict will undoubtedly rear its head at some point. Having a shared goal can help these teams work more effectively through conflict to reach a mutually beneficial resolution.
