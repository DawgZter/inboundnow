## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees by enabling access to a wider range of providers and specialists, as well as significantly reducing wait times.
In Australia, Remote provides both international and local coverage plans, offering comprehensive health insurance benefits. When extending this benefit to your team in Australia, Remote makes two options available for enrollment by employees:
• Local Coverage Plan: Specifically designed to meet Australia’s regulatory health insurance requirements, ensuring full compliance and local support.
• International Coverage Plan: Offers extensive health benefits that extend beyond local boundaries, ideal for employees who may require medical services while abroad or prefer a broader scope of healthcare options.

## Local Coverage

The BUPA local health coverage is designed to remove or reduce the costs required for treatment in hospitals.

### Hospital Cover

• Rehabilitation, psychiatric services, and palliative care
• Brain, nervous system, and blood treatments
• Cancer treatments (chemotherapy, radiotherapy, immunotherapy)
• Eye (excluding cataracts), ear, nose, and throat
• Bone, joint, and muscle procedures, including joint replacements and reconstructions
• Kidney, bladder, and chronic kidney failure dialysis
• Digestive system, hernia, appendix, and gastrointestinal endoscopy
• Weight loss surgery, heart, and vascular system treatments
• Lung and chest, gynaecology, miscarriage, and pregnancy care
• Diabetes management (excluding insulin pumps), pain management
• Medically necessary breast surgery, plastic and reconstructive surgery
• Skin, dental surgery, sleep studies, podiatric surgery

### Medical Cover

• Inpatient medical costs, diagnostic tests (x-ray, pathology)
• GP and specialist consultations, allied health services
• Selected pharmacy items

### Emergency Services

• Uncapped emergency ambulance services
• Limited non-emergency ambulance services
• Emergency department fees if admitted
