## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees by enabling access to a wider range of providers and specialists, as well as significantly reducing wait times. With two comprehensive plans – the Integral Plan and the Integral Plus Plan – designed to cater to diverse needs, with exclusive dental coverage available on the Integral Plus Plan. Learn more about Remote’s health insurance offering below. If you would like more insight into fair equity and benefits best practices, you can download our Global Benefits Guide here !

-

## Inpatient Coverage

- 100% coverage without deductibles or limits on medical services.

- Unlimited medical and surgical treatment.

- Unlimited ICU hospitalisation.

- Private individual room.

- Bed for a companion and nurse for users under 15 and over 65 years old without any limit on days.

- Unlimited coverage for all specialties within the hospital without additional charges.

- Up to 50 days of psychiatric hospitalisation.

- Reversible dialysis up to 8 sessions per hospitalisation event.

- Unlimited prostheses and osteosynthesis material.

- Delivery or caesarean section.

## Outpatient Coverage

- Unlimited out-of-network care up to COP 200,000 per visit (less the value of the copayment) for:

- Alternative medical consultation (acupuncture, bioenergetics, homoeopathy, osteopathy, and chiropractic).

- Psychological and psychiatric consultation.

- General practitioner and specialist consultation.

- Unlimited in-network care:

- Direct access to primary care physicians and all specialists.

- Direct payment of charges at the doctor’s office.

- Clinical laboratory tests without prior authorization.

- Unlimited simple and specialised diagnostic exams.

- Unlimited therapy sessions.

- Nationwide emergency care.

- Home medical consultations.

## Dental Coverage

Not covered

## Vision Coverage

Not covered

## Additional Benefits

- Medical care and assistance abroad (up to USD 30,000) with prior authorization 24 hours before the trip.

- Prosthetics and osteosynthesis materials up to 60 times the legal minimum monthly wage (SMMLV).

- Home medical assistance through Emermedica included.

- Tele-consultation and telemedicine, including psychological and nutritional telephone counselling: Unlimited in-network.

- Cancer treatment includes unlimited commercial oncology medications for chemotherapy, both outpatient and inpatient, as well as adjuvant drugs.

- Treatment for HIV/AIDS and its complications.

- Telephone counselling and veterinary medicine for pets (5 consultations per year, free of charge).

## Additional Benefits

- Life Insurance:

Coverage extends to 12 months of the quota for a Colsanitas Integral plan for dependents in the event of the employee's death or permanent disability.

- Exequial Benefit:

Comprehensive funeral services provided at no additional cost, alleviating financial and logistical burdens for families.

- Travel Assistance:

Medical emergency care available worldwide with coverage up to $35,000 (or €35,000 in the European community) and dental emergency coverage up to $500.
