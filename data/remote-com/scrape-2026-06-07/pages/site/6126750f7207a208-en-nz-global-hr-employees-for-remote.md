## Frequently asked questions

Who will my employment contract be with?

Your employment contract will be with the local entity of Remote where you are based. For example, if you are employed in Singapore, then your employment contract will be with our legal entity Remote Tech Singapore Pte. Ltd. Remote employs you locally as the employer of record on behalf of the company you work for. All the business operations and day-to-day of your work is handled by the company you work for. In order to do this, Remote takes on all of the responsibilities, obligations and tasks related to your employment.

How will I get my pay?

Where possible, we use local banks to transfer your pay to you via methods like domestic wire transfers, ACH, or SEPA. If local bank transfers are not available to us in your country and your local currency, you will receive your pay via international wire transfer (SWIFT).

What currency will I receive my pay in?

You will receive your pay in the local currency outlined in your employment contract.

When will I get my pay?

Bank transfers for salaries are made on the 25th of each month - you will receive your pay on or before the last working day of each month.

Why is my pay payment in the name of Remote?

As the Employer of Record, Remote is your local legal employer, and every pay payment will be made by us. The operations and day-to-day of your work is still handled by the company you work for.

Will I be covered by social security programs in my location?

Yes - Remote’s local entity contributes to and enrols you in local social security programs as well as other statutory requirements.

What benefits will I receive?

The benefits you receive will depend on the selection made by your employer. This may include private health insurance, bonuses and commissions, or other custom perks.

Can Remote sponsor my visa?

Remote offers advice on laws that could impact your ability to work in certain countries and what employment options are available for employees. Remote is able to sponsor work visas in some locations. Your employer can find out more and initiate the process through our support team.
