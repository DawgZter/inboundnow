## Employing in Bahamas

Provisions for employee protections and workers’ rights are spelled out in the Bahamian Constitution as well as several government statutes, such as the Employment Act of 2001, the Fair Labor Standards Act, and the Health and Safety at Work Act of 2002.
Employees in the Bahamas generally enjoy protection against discrimination based on age, sexual orientation, gender, and race.

Minimum wage

The minimum wage in the Bahamas is currently fixed at $5.25 per hour, $42 per daily, or roughly $210 per week.
The Bahamian dollar is pegged to the US dollar so those figures represent what a Bahamian would earn in U.S. dollars.

## Facts & Stats

The Bahamas has been continuously inhabited since the 9th century AD when the Lucayans migrated to the island paradise from nearby Cuba, roughly seven centuries before Columbus arrived in the Caribbean. Flash forward two centuries later and the archipelago gained independence from its British colonial owners in 1973.
In modern times, the nation of just under 400,000 boasts one of the strongest economies in the Carribean, buoyed by a flourishing tourism industry that welcomes over 7 million globetrotters each year.
The Bahamas remains popular among corporate professionals and companies looking to reduce taxation levels, as well as a growing group of digital nomads looking to explore the world.
