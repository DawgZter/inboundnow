## Wellness

Wellness Benefit

Clients can choose to offer the Wellness & Lifestyle Benefit and select from multiple contribution levels, providing their team members a monthly wallet, which they can use with their virtual card at approved stores, online retailers, or any other approved vendors related to Lifestyle and Wellness benefits. The offered services include:

- Physical and mental health support such as gym memberships, fitness classes, therapy, mindfulness apps, and guided meditation.

- Lifestyle services like streaming subscriptions (Spotify, Netflix), groceries, and other services that support work-life balance and personal well-being.

- Personalised recommendations to help employees select benefits most relevant to their needs.

- Platform support for employees needing immediate assistance or guidance on wellness-related concerns.
