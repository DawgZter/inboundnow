## Learn more about HR document management

What types of documents can I manage with Remote?

You can build any type of document or contract and format it to fit your needs. You can also add custom fields to be filled in later and add a time-stamped signature block for recording acceptance.

Who can sign a document on behalf of the company?

The company admin that creates a document can sign the signature block on behalf of the company. They can also choose to add a different person as signatory.

The signatory must be an admin with the right permissions that allow them to sign contracts and documents on behalf of the company.

Where can I view signed and finalised documents?

Admins can view them from the Documents tab in the platform. The can also go to an employee’s profile and view them on the Files tab.

Employees and contractors can view theirs in the Documents tab in their Remote account.

What type of signature can be captured on a document?

If a signature block is added to a document, both a signatory for the company and the employee or contractor can sign it. They simply type in their name and a digital signature with a timestamp is added to the document.
