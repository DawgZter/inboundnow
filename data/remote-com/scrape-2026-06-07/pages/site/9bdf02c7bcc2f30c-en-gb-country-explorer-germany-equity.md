## Stock options for employees in Germany

Equity incentives are an invaluable way to attract, motivate, and retain top talent for your business. But when you cross hiring borders, they can become deeply complex.

Remote enables you to easily offer non-qualified stock options (NSOs) to your team members in Germany. There are no compliance headaches or administrative hassles — just simplicity and clarity for you and your people at every step.

### What are NSOs?

NSOs are a type of equity incentive. They give your team members the right to buy a set number of shares in your company at a fixed price, known as the exercise price.

This typically happens after a vesting period, which is often based on the length of time your team member stays at your company. As a result, they are a great way to foster long-term commitment, and align people with your company’s strategic goals.

Benefit 1: Taxation deferral

The Future Financing Act (“ZuFinG”) allows — under certain conditions — the deferral of taxes on an employees' stock options until sale. However, this deferral is not applicable if the shares granted are the shares of a parent company outside Germany (even for German employees of a subsidiary of that parent company).
Benefit 2: Limited exemption on the spread

In some cases, a portion of the spread can be tax-free. A set amount for each employee can be deducted from the taxable income, reducing how much tax they owe.
