## Employing in Tunisia

Workers' rights in Tunisia are protected by:

- The Tunisian Constitution of 2014

- The Labour Code of 1966

- Decree 95-539 of 1995 on the functioning of regional councils and the central arbitration board in trade disputes

— all of which guarantee equal pay for equal work, the right to individual and collective labour disputes, safe working environments, and protections against discrimination based on age, religion, and race.
Common questions that could come up during the hiring process include the minimum wage, overtime rates, and guaranteed paid time off. Remote can help you offer a complete, competitive, and compliant benefits package to your employees in Tunisia.

Minimum Wage

The minimum wage in Tunisia is TND 448,238.00 per month or TND 5,378,856.00 per year, based on a standard 40-hour workweek. For a 48-hour workweek, the minimum wage is TND 528,320.00 per month or TND 6,339,840.00 per year. These rates are effective starting January 1, 2025.

## Facts & Stats

Tunisia is the perfect blend of North African Saharan landscape, Mediterranean influences, and Middle Eastern culture. It boasts a growing economy powered by exports of electrical hardware, olives, textiles, citrus, agricultural products, oil, and vehicles
The country has a sizable GDP of over $136 billion and one of the fastest internet speeds in the MENA region. In fact, 51% of the labor force is employed in service industries, and it has the 6th highest number of developers in Africa — tellingly, Tunisia will be a major talent hotspot as the world embraces remote work.
