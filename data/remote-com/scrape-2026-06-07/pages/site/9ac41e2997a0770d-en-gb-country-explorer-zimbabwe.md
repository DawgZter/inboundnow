## Employing in Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe’s employment laws and regulations are laid out in the country’s Labour Act of 1985. The most recent amendment was passed in 2016.
Working hours in the commercial sector may not exceed 45 hours per week, and 9 hours per day. There is no minimum prescribed amount of working hours.

Minimum wage

There is currently no minimum wage in Zimbabwe as they vary across sectors. The minimum wage is set per industry and job type and ranges from 295 USD - 543 USD per month.
The commercial sector has recently updated its minimum wage to 3540 USD - 6516 USD per year.

## Facts & Stats

Known primarily for its diverse natural landscapes and rich biodiversity, the Republic of Zimbabwe is a presidential republic with democratic elections.
Its strategic location in southern Africa provides businesses with opportunities to tap into regional markets, with Zimbabwe itself home to a well-educated and skilled workforce.
In recent years, the country has undergone numerous economic reforms and, as part of this effort, the government has introduced several investment-friendly initiatives aimed at streamlining bureaucratic processes and promoting foreign investment. As a result, Zimbabwe is an ideal location for organisations looking to expand and hire in the region.
