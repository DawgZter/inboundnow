# Contractor Conversion

Want your company to convert you from a contractor to a full-time employee, even if you live in a different country? Remote can help!

Nominate your employer

Nominate your employer using the form. Remote will reach out to your company with information on how to convert you from a contractor to an employee. This nomination is anonymous, so your employer will not receive your name unless they explicitly ask who nominated them.
