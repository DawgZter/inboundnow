## Accident & Disability

-

Short & Long Term Disability

## Short-Term Disability (STD)

- Coverage:

- Provides benefits after 7 consecutive days of absence from work.

- Replaces 60% of your weekly earnings, up to $2,400 per week.

- Benefits last for up to 12 weeks.

## Long-Term Disability (LTD)

- Coverage:

- Begins after a 90-day waiting period, which matches the end of the STD benefit period.

- Replaces 60% of your monthly pay, up to $11,000 per month.

- Benefits continue until maximum Social Security Normal Retirement Age or until recovery.
