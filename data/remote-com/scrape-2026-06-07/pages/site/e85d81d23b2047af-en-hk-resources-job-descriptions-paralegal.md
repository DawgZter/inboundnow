# Paralegal job description

A paralegal supports legal teams by conducting research, drafting documents, and managing case files. This role is essential for maintaining legal accuracy, streamlining operations, and ensuring compliance within an organisation.

### About the role

As a paralegal at [Company], you will work closely with attorneys and other legal professionals to support a variety of legal functions. From preparing legal documents to conducting research and managing filings, you will be an integral part of keeping legal processes efficient and compliant.

### Paralegal responsibilities

Your responsibilities will include:

- Conducting legal research and summarising findings for attorneys.

- Drafting contracts, pleadings, and other legal documents.

- Organising and maintaining legal files, records, and databases.

- Assisting with case preparation and document review.

- Coordinating with internal departments and external parties.

- Managing deadlines and court or regulatory filings.

- Supporting compliance and corporate governance tasks as needed.

### Paralegal qualifications

For this role, you will need:

- A paralegal certificate or degree in legal studies, law, or a related field.

- Previous experience in a legal or administrative support role.

- Familiarity with legal terminology, documents, and processes.

- Proficiency in legal research tools (e.g., LexisNexis, Westlaw).

- Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.

- Excellent communication and time management abilities.

### Paralegal skills

- Legal research and writing

- Contract drafting and review

- Case management and documentation

- Familiarity with legal databases and filing systems

- Communication and collaboration

- Deadline and task tracking

- Discretion and confidentiality

## Paths to becoming a paralegal

Most paralegals start by completing a paralegal certification or a degree in legal studies. Many gain experience in law firms, corporate legal departments, or government agencies. Attention to detail, an understanding of legal systems, and administrative capabilities are essential. Specialised certifications (e.g., NALA or NFPA) can further enhance qualifications and career potential.

## Typical career progression for a paralegal

Paralegals can often progress to one or several of the following roles:

- Senior paralegal: Manages complex cases and oversees junior team members.

- Legal operations specialist: Focuses on systems and tools that support legal workflows.

- Contract manager: Oversees the drafting and negotiation of company contracts.

- Compliance officer: Ensures adherence to legal and regulatory requirements.

- Legal project manager: Coordinates large legal initiatives across teams.

## Other possible titles for this role

- Legal analyst

- Legal coordinator

- Legal operations associate

- Contract administrator
