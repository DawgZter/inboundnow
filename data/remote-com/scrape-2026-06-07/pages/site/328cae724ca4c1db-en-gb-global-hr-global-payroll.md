## Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I start running global payroll with Remote?

Depending on your circumstances, you can be processing payroll for your team in weeks - compared to months with other global payroll providers. You’ll be assigned a personal implementation specialist to guide you through the process and build the plan together.

In which countries does Remote support global payroll and payments?

You can use Remote to run payroll around the world in top countries for tech talent, including the UK, US, Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Mexico, Ireland, South Africa, Indonesia, India, and dozens more.

We operate payroll in-house, with our local experts ensuring your compliance with local payroll rules and regulations. We don’t use third-party providers, providing you with greater flexibility, speed, and support.

We’re adding new countries every month. Get in touch with our sales team to find out how we can support your global payroll needs.

How do you protect sensitive employee data?

Remote protects your business and your employees’ sensitive data with industry-leading security and compliance. We're SOC 2 compliant and ISO27001 certified, ensuring your valuable data is secure.

Remote requires two-factor authentication (2FA) and offers single sign-on (SSO) integration, adding extra layers of user security.

Our security policies and procedures are also regularly audited and verified by independent third-party auditors. Find out more about security and data protection at Remote.

Can Remote manage payments to employees and tax authorities in each country?

Yes, in some countries, Remote can automatically pay your employees and tax authorities using standard bank payment methods.

Once you've completed our thorough and secure payments onboarding process, you can fund your virtual payroll wallet via bank transfer. After you approve the monthly payroll, we'll securely send payments to your employees and tax authorities in local currencies. You'll have complete visibility into the status of each payment.

For all countries, you also have the option to download a 'bank file' that can be uploaded to your banking software for easy scheduling of pay, benefit, and tax payments, if you prefer not to use our integrated payment network.

Find out more about Remote Payroll payments .

Do I need a local entity to pay employees in another country, or can Remote act as Employer of Record?

Paying employees in another country typically requires your business to:

-
Have a local business entity

-
Maintain a local business account

-
Register with local tax and labour authorities

If you don’t have these, consider hiring employees through our Employer of Record service, which allows companies to hire international employees without local entities. An Employer of Record manages everything from hiring and compliance to payroll, benefits, and more for your foreign employees — freeing you to focus on your business.
