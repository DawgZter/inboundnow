## Employee Assistance Program

Remote offers a comprehensive and optional Employee Assistance Program (EAP) for your workforce in Canada. This program provides round-the-clock support and resources to help employees manage a wide range of personal and professional challenges. The EAP extends to dependents and family members, ensuring holistic support for your team. With access to counseling, legal and financial advice, and wellbeing resources, this program is designed to enhance the overall wellbeing of your team members and their families.

-

You also have the option to offer an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) to your workforce. This includes:

- 24/7/365 telephone support help line

- Wellbeing platform & mobile app (available in 50 languages)

- Employee legal help line

- Consulting services for financial, debt advice, housing, relocation, parenting, eldercare, health and wellness, and more

- Dependent & family access (up to 5 per employee)

- Up to 5 counselling sessions per issue (face-to-face, video, or structured telephone counselling sessions)

- And much more!
