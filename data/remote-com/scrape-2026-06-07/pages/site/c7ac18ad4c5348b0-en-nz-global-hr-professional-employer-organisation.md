## Frequently Asked Questions

What is a PEO?

A PEO, or Professional Employer Organisation, provides comprehensive HR solutions for businesses, including payroll, benefits, and compliance management.

How does co-employment work?

In a co-employment arrangement, the PEO becomes the employer of record for tax and insurance purposes, while you maintain control over your employees' day-to-day activities.

Is my business eligible for PEO services?

Most small to mid-sized businesses can benefit from PEO services. Contact us to see how we can assist your specific needs.

What are the benefits of using a PEO?

PEOs help reduce administrative burden, lower HR costs, improve compliance, offer better benefits, and allow you to focus on growing your business.

Will I lose control over my employees?

Not at all. You retain full control over hiring, firing, compensation decisions, and daily operations—while we handle HR, payroll, and compliance behind the scenes.

How can a PEO help with compliance and risk management?

We help you stay ahead of constantly changing employment laws and regulations, reducing your risk of lawsuits, audits, and penalties.

Can I offer better benefits through a PEO?

Yes—PEOs pool employees across clients, giving your business access to high-quality health plans and retirement options usually reserved for large corporations.

How does billing work with a PEO?

Our pricing is transparent and based on a flat fee per employee. No hidden fees or surprise charges.

How quickly can we get started with PEO services?

We’ll work with you to align onboarding with your anticipated start date, ensuring readiness based on your team’s timeline and requirements.

How do I know if a PEO is right for my company?

If you're spending too much time on HR admin, facing compliance concerns, or want to offer better benefits, a PEO could be a game-changer. Let’s talk.
