## Types of leave in France

Statutory leave

All workers are legally entitled to 30 days' paid holiday leave a year (including Saturdays). In addition, full-time workers have 11 paid public holidays a year. The minimum amount of annual leave is 5 weeks.

Pregnancy and maternity leave

The length of pregnancy and maternity leave varies from 16 to 46 weeks. This depends on how many children the mother has, and how many children are birthed at the same time.

Paternity leave

The length of paternity leave varies from 25 to 32 days depending on the number of children birthed.

Adoption leave

The length of adoption leave varies from 16 to 22 weeks (and 32 days for fathers) depending on the number of children adopted, and if leave is shared between parents or not.
