# SEO specialist: job overview

At [Your company name], the SEO Specialist plays a central role in strengthening our online presence and ensuring our websites are optimised for search engines. The position covers strategic planning, execution and ongoing monitoring of SEO initiatives to improve rankings, increase traffic and enhance user engagement.

## About the company

[Your company name] is a [brief description of your company, e.g., 'leading provider of innovative tech solutions']. We are committed to keeping pace with technology and marketing trends to deliver the best possible outcomes for our clients.

## Key responsibilities

- Carry out comprehensive SEO audits on our digital content.

- Create and put into practice SEO strategies that align with our business objectives.

- Work with content creators to embed SEO best practices throughout content production.

- Monitor and analyse SEO performance, adjusting strategies as required.

## Qualifications

- Bachelor’s degree in marketing, information technology or a related discipline.

- Demonstrable experience in SEO or an equivalent digital marketing role.

- Experience with SEO and keyword research tools (for example Google Analytics, SEMrush, Moz).

## Key skills

- Strong analytical ability to interpret complex data and metrics.

- Proficient in SEO practices and keyword optimisation.

- Excellent problem‑solving skills and creativity to discover new approaches for improving website visibility.

- Excellent communication skills for effective cross‑team collaboration.

Note:

SEO Specialists play a crucial role in driving organic growth and increasing the visibility of the company’s digital assets. Their expertise attracts more traffic while improving user experience and engagement with the brand.

### How to become an SEO Specialist

- Build foundational knowledge through study in marketing or related areas.

- Obtain practical experience via internships or entry‑level roles in digital marketing.

- Keep up to date with the latest SEO trends and search algorithm updates.

- Digital Marketing Manager
