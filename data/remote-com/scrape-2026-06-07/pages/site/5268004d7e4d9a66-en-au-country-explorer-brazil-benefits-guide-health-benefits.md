## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees by enabling access to a wider range of providers and specialists, as well as significantly reducing wait times. Remote offers comprehensive health benefits to employees in Brazil through our Local Standard, Local Premium, and Local Prestige plans. When onboarding a new hire in Brazil, Remote provides several plan options, including the Local Standard, Local Premium, and Local Prestige plans. The Local Standard plan covers essential health services such as emergency care, inpatient and outpatient hospitalization, childbirth, doctor appointments, elective procedures, exams, tests, treatments, therapies, unlimited psychotherapy and psychiatry sessions, and specific transplants. The Local Premium plan includes all the benefits of the Standard plan with a higher reimbursement limit, access to unique medical facilities, and enhanced travel assistance. The Local Prestige plan provides the most extensive coverage, including additional services like home physiotherapy and medical appointments, no degree limit for refractive surgery, the highest reimbursement limit, and comprehensive travel assistance. If you would like more insight into fair equity and benefits best practices, you can download our Global Benefits Guide here !

## Family Coverage

Employees can cover the cost of dependents at their own expense.
