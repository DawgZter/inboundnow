## Papaya vs Remote FAQs

What are the key differences between Papaya and Remote?

Papaya is a specialist global payroll software provider, while Remote is a dedicated global employment solution, offering HR and recruitment tools that Papaya does not. Papaya relies on third-party partners for payroll, benefits, and tax management, whereas Remote owns legal entities in each country of operation, providing greater control and compliance.

How does Papaya pricing compare to Remote?

Remote’s Employer of Record (EOR) service is priced at $699/month per employee, while contractor management costs just $29/month. Papaya’s prices are comparable, but you’re likely to pay additional third-party costs and transaction fees. Remote’s fair price guarantee ensures maximum cost-efficiency and transparency.

Is Papaya better than Remote for global payroll services?

While Papaya Global is a leading global payroll provider that operates in multiple countries, it doesn’t own and manage all its legal entities — instead relying on third-party outsourcing. Remote operates a 100% owned entity infrastructure, and is rated as a superior multi-country payroll provider to Papaya based on G2 reviews.

Remote’s ownership of legal entities also enhances service reliability and consistency across regions.
