## Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between base salary and total compensation?

"Base salary" is the core income an employee receives, not including bonuses, benefits, or any other financial incentives. In comparison, total compensation encompasses the base salary plus all other perks and benefits, such as health insurance, stock options, and performance bonuses, providing a full picture of an employee's earnings.

Both figures are crucial for employers to stay competitive and for employees to make informed decisions.

The Salary Explorer on Remote provides only base salary information. Our team are on-hand to offer personalized advice on benefits, equity, and incentives if you need support.

How does the Salary Explorer support salary transparency initiatives?

With access to transparent salary data from Remote, you can mitigate pay disparities and encourages a more equitable workplace culture.

Can I see median salary figures in the Salary Explorer?

Yes, the Salary Explorer provides "median salary" figures, which represent the middle value in a list of salaries for a given role and region — half of the data points are higher, and half are lower. This statistic is essential for understanding the standard compensation for a role, helping both employers and job seekers make decisions based on accurate and current market conditions.

How can I find the annual salary for a specific role using the Salary Explorer?

To find the annual salary for any role, enter the job title and location into the Salary Explorer. The platform provides a comprehensive breakdown of salary data including starting, median, and top-end figures, which are vital for employers setting new salaries and for employees during negotiations.

How does the Salary Explorer integrate with Remote's Global HR platform?

The Salary Explorer is fully integrated into Remote's global HR platform, allowing HR professionals to seamlessly access global compensation data alongside other HR functions such as onboarding, payroll, and compliance management. This integration helps streamline multiple HR processes, ensuring that users can manage all their global HR needs in one efficient platform.

Can the Salary Explorer help with compliance relating to global compensation regulations?

The Salary Explorer gives you context on global salary ranges, but navigating the complex landscape of international labor laws can be difficult.

Companies work with Remote Payroll, Contractor Management, and Employer of Record to ensure compliance with minimum wage laws, overtime regulations, and other legal requirements across different countries.

How does using the Salary Explorer improve strategic decision-making in HR?

By providing real-time, accurate salary data, the Salary Explorer empowers HR leaders to make strategic decisions that align with both business objectives and employee expectations. This can lead to improved employee retention, more efficient allocation of HR budgets, and better positioning of the company as a competitive employer in the global market.
