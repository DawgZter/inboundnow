# Improving Life-Work Balance for Your Remote Workforce

Watch four experts from Remote as they share practical strategies for managers and people leaders at every level to protect remote team members against burnout.

## How to manage work burnout

We discuss the importance of life-work balance for remote work, featuring panelists from Remote. It covers the challenges of maintaining work-life balance while working remotely, the signs of poor work-life balance and potential burnout.
