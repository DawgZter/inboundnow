## Voluntary Coverages

Remote offers a variety of voluntary coverages that employees can opt to include in their benefits package for additional financial security. These coverages are fully employee-paid and include options such as Voluntary Life and AD&D Insurance, Critical Illness Insurance, Hospital Insurance, and Accident Insurance. These options provide employees with the flexibility to enhance their coverage based on their individual needs.

-

Additional Voluntary Coverages

## Voluntary Life and AD&D Insurance

### Employee Coverage

- Increments: $10,000

- Maximum: Up to $1,000,000

- Guarantee Issue: $200,000 (for new hires or first-time eligible employees)

- Benefit Reduction: 35% at age 65, 50% at age 70

### Spouse Coverage

- Increments: $5,000 (employee must be enrolled in Voluntary Employee Life)

- Maximum: Up to $100,000

- Guarantee Issue: $30,000 (initial offer only)

### Child Benefits

$10,000 (flat option, employee must be enrolled in Voluntary Employee Life)

## Critical Illness Insurance

### Coverage Basics

- Lump-sum payment upon diagnosis of a specified critical illness.

- Helps with expenses not covered by health insurance.

- Flexible use of funds for medical costs or other expenses.

### Coverage Amounts

Employees can elect amounts in increments of $5,000 to $30,000 (spouse up to 50% of employee amount).

## Hospital Insurance

### Coverage Basics

- Provides financial benefits for covered hospital stays.

- Complements major medical insurance by covering additional out-of-pocket costs.

- Cash benefits provided directly unless otherwise assigned.

### Coverage Amounts

- Hospital/ICU Admission: $1,500 per admission (up to 2 admissions per insured)

- Hospital Confinement: $100/$200 per day (max of 15 days per insured per year)

## Accident Insurance

### Coverage Basics

- Covers treatment fees for accidents not fully covered by medical insurance.

- Cash benefits provided directly unless otherwise assigned.

- Can be used for various services such as ambulance rates, diagnostics, intensive care, therapy, and rehabilitation costs.
