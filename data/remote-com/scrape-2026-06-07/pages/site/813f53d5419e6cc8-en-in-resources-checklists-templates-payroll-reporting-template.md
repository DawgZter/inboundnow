# Payroll reporting template

Use this payroll reporting template to generate accurate, compliant payroll reports for your US-based employees. Whether you’re a growing startup in the states or a scaling global business, this structure helps you stay on top of payroll taxes, employee pay summaries, and reporting obligations.

## What is a payroll report?

A payroll report is a comprehensive summary of wages, tax withholdings, and contributions for a specific pay period. It helps you:

- Ensure compliance with federal and state requirements

- Track payroll costs and employee compensation

- Support end-of-year reporting and audits

- Verify payments to employees and government agencies

### 5. Documents to generate and file

Ensure you generate and file the following documents and forms:

- Employee pay stubs

- IRS Form 941 (Quarterly Federal Tax Return)

- IRS Form 940 (Annual FUTA Tax Return)

- State unemployment tax returns (varies by state)

- State/local income tax filings

- W-2 forms (annually)

If necessary, check your state labour department for specific filing schedules.
See also: The employer’s guide to payroll tax forms in the US

## Common mistakes to avoid

Some of the most common mistakes employers make when reporting payroll include:

- Missing SUTA registrations in remote employee states

- Filing Form 941 late (or with incorrect tax amounts)

- Underreporting pre-tax deductions like 401(k) or HSA

- Not updating payroll reports after employee status changes
