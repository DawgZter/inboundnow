## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees by enabling access to a wider range of providers and specialists, as well as significantly reducing wait times. Switzerland’s healthcare system includes both a universal public system and a private system. It is globally renowned, with highly qualified doctors and short wait times. To access it, residents are required to have at least basic health insurance, paid for through individual contributions. Many residents also have supplementary private health insurance, typically provided through their employers. In Switzerland, Remote offers two health insurance options: the Supplemental Health Insurance Plan, which enhances the existing personal Swiss health insurance plan and provides wider and more flexible healthcare options, and the Full Local Health Insurance Plan, which combines the supplemental coverage with the mandatory Swiss base cover, ensuring comprehensive healthcare protection and meeting Swiss governmental requirements for health insurance. ## In-Patient Benefits

The Supplemental offering is designed to enhance the existing personal Swiss health insurance plan, providing wider and more flexible healthcare options. It supplements the mandatory Swiss coverage with additional benefits.

### Coverage Limit: 1,462,500 CHF

- Hospital Accommodations: Semi-private room

- Intensive Care: Full refund

- Prescription Drugs: Full refund

- Surgical Fees: Full refund

- Diagnostic Tests: Full refund

- Organ Transplants: Full refund

- Emergency In-patient Dental: Full refund

- Out-patient Surgery: Full refund

- Local Ambulance: Full refund

- CT & MRI Scans: Full refund

- Oncology: Full refund

- Routine Maternity: Full refund

- Complications in Pregnancy & Childbirth: Full refund

- Palliative Care: Full refund

- Psychiatry and Psychotherapy : 13,780 CHF

- Medical evacuation : Full refund

- Expenses for one person accompanying an evacuated person: 3,900 CHF

- Travel costs of insured family members in the event of an evacuation : 2,600 CHF

- Repatriation of mortal remains: 13,000 CHF

- Long term care: Full refund

## Out-Patient Benefits

### Coverage Limit: 16,575 CHF

- Medical Practitioner Fees & Prescription Drugs: Full refund

- Specialist Fees: Full refund

- Diagnostic Tests: Full refund

- Vaccinations: Full refund

- Chiropractic Treatment, Osteopathy, Homoeopathy, Chinese Herbal Medicine, & Acupuncture: Full refund (max. 12 sessions per condition)

- Prescribed Physiotherapy: Full refund (max. 12 sessions per condition, 5 sessions for non-prescribed physiotherapy)

- Health & Wellbeing Checks Incl. Screening For Early Detection of Disease: 780 CHF

- Infertility Treatment: 15,600 CHF (lifetime)

- Psychiatry and Psychotherapy: 20 visits

- Prescribed medical aids: 3,250 CHF

## Dental Benefits

### Coverage Limit: 2,665 CHF

- Dental Treatment: 80% refund

- Dental Surgery: 80% refund

- Periodontics: 80% refund

- Orthodontic Treatment & Dental Prosthetics: 50% refund

## Vision Benefits

## Local Swiss Plan

- Hospitalisation: Acceptance of all costs in the general ward of a hospital

- Outpatient - Conventional Medicine: Costs covered according to recognised tariffs

- Outpatient - Complementary Medicine: Acupuncture, anthroposophic medicine, homoeopathy, physiotherapy, and traditional Chinese medicine carried out by a doctor with a Swiss Federal Diploma and professional qualifications are covered
