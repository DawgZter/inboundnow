## Health Benefits

In India, it is mandatory for employers to provide health insurance to employees.
When onboarding a new team member in India, Remote provides comprehensive health benefits through our local offering, which includes six tiered plan options , ensuring you can find the right fit for your team. These plans offer sum insured amounts of ₹2,000,000, ₹3,000,000, or ₹4,000,000 per family. While all plans cover the employee, spouse, and up to four children, we also offer specific options that extend coverage to parents or parents-in-law.
If you would like more insight into fair equity and benefits best practices, you can download our Global Benefits Guide here !

-

Platinum

## Inpatient Coverage

- Sum Insured: ₹2,000,000 ($24,100) per family

- Maternity Cover: ₹200,000 ($2,400) for the first 4 children

- Room Rent Limits: No limit

- Daycare Treatments: Covered

- Pre-existing Diseases: Covered from Day 1

- Modern Treatments (e.g., Cyber-knife, Stem Cell): Covered up to Sum Insured, with 50% co-pay

- Mental Illness Hospitalisation: ₹100,000 ($1,200) within Sum Insured

- COVID-19 Treatments: Covered up to Sum Insured

- Internal Congenital Disease: Covered up to Sum Insured

- External Congenital Disease: Covered in life-threatening situations

- Psychiatric Ailments: ₹100,000 ($1,200) within Sum Insured

- Organ Donor Expenses: Covered if the organ is for the insured’s use; the cost of the organ is not covered

- Gender Affirming Therapies and Surgeries: Covered up to ₹300,000 ($3,600) within Sum Insured

- LGBTQ Cover: Covered

## Outpatient Coverage

- Coverage Limit: ₹40,000 ($480) per family

- Premium Doctor Consultations: Unlimited online doctor consults with 20+ specialities

- Mental Health: Unlimited online consultations with psychologists and psychiatrists

- Nutrition Counselling: Unlimited online consultations with expert nutritionists

- Veterinary Care: Unlimited online consultations with top veterinarians for your pets

- Vision (Spectacles and Lenses): Covered

- Dental Treatments (Fillings, Root Canals, Scaling and Cleaning, Tooth Extraction, Replacement): Covered

- Vaccinations including COVID Vaccination: Covered

- Physiotherapy, Nursing care post-hospitalisation, AYUSH treatments, Prescribed diagnostics, Medicines, Health Checkups, Minor surgeries and dressing

## Vision Coverage

- Cataract: Covered

- Lasik Surgery: Covered if correction index is +/- 6.5D

- Lucentis: Covered up to ₹50,000 ($600)

## Dental Coverage

- Dental Treatments: Covered under outpatient cover

- Consultations and care: Covered

## Family Coverage

Members Covered: Employee, Spouse, up to 4 children
