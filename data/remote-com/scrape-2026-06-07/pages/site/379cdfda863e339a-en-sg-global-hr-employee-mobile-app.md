# Employee Mobile App

Give your employees the power to manage time off, track expenses, and view their payslips on the go.

Download on the App Store

Download on Google Play

## Track time-off requests on the go

Whether your employees are planning a holiday or at home sick, they can keep their records up-to-date through the app – ready for their manager to review and approve in Remote.

Employees and contractors can:

Check how much time off they've used

Request and edit time-off requests in seconds

Log public holidays, sick leave, and more for complete transparency

## Submit expenses with the snap of a receipt

There's no need for your employees to deal with a mountain of expense paperwork at the end of each month.

Take a photo of a receipt or invoice

Our image recognition will recognise your document automatically. You can also upload photos or PDFs.

Review the expense details

Smart image recognition technology extracts key details like the company, price, and date of the transaction — saving time and improving accuracy.

Track expenses through approvals and payment

Employees see the status update as their manager and/or people/finance team approve, process, and pay the expense request.
