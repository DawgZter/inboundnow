## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees by enabling access to a wider range of providers and specialists, as well as significantly reducing wait times. When onboarding a new hire in Ireland, Remote offers the Local Health Guide 2 and Local Health Guide 3 plans. The Local Health Guide 2 plan provides extensive inpatient and outpatient coverage, including semi-private hospital rooms, emergency in-patient treatment abroad, psychiatric treatment, minor injury clinic cover, routine and non-routine dental treatments, vision coverage, and additional benefits like laser treatments and mental health support. The Local Health Guide 3 plan enhances these benefits with more extensive outpatient services, additional dental and vision care, and higher coverage limits for various treatments. If you would like more insight into fair equity and benefits best practices, you can download our Global Benefits Guide here !
