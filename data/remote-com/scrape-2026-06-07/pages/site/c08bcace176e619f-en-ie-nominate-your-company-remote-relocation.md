# Remote Relocation

Want to remain employed at your current company while relocating to another country? Want your company to convert you from a contractor to a full-time employee, even if you live in a different country? Remote can help!

Nominate your employer

Nominate your employer using the form. Remote will reach out to your company about Remote Relocation services. This nomination is anonymous, so your employer will not receive your name unless they explicitly ask who nominated them.
