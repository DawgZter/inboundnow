## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees. Remote offers comprehensive health benefits in Portugal, through our Local Basic, Standard, and Premium plans. When onboarding a new hire in Portugal, different health benefits plans are available which include in-network and out-of-network coverage for various medical services, emergency services, dental care, prostheses and orthoses (including vision insurance), and coverage for serious illnesses. The Local Basic plan covers essential health services with co-payment options, including inpatient and outpatient care, medical appointments, emergency services, and physiotherapy. The Local Standard plan provides similar benefits with additional coverage for prostheses and orthoses, including vision insurance. The Local Premium plan offers the highest level of coverage, including more extensive dental benefits, serious illness coverage, and a higher insured amount for comprehensive medical services. When health insurance benefits are offered, they must be provided equally to all employees in the respective country. If you would like more insight into fair equity and benefits best practices, you can download our Global Benefits Guide here !
