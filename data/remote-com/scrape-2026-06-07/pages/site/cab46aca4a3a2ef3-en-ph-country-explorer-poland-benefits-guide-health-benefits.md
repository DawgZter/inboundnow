## Health Benefits

Offering health insurance in this country is not mandatory, but providing these additional health benefits can greatly enhance the healthcare experience for employees by enabling access to a wider range of providers and specialists, as well as significantly reducing wait times. In Poland, Remote offers both local and international coverage options to ensure that clients hiring in the country have the flexibility and coverage needed to provide the best possible care for employees. The international plans can be used both globally (excluding the US) and locally, ensuring comprehensive coverage wherever employees are, while the local plans only cover services locally but enjoy the benefits of a more extensive local network and specialized care. When onboarding a new hire, Remote offers several plan options: the International Standard plan, the International Premium plan, the International Gold plan, the International Platinum plan, the Local Standard plan, the Local Comprehensive Care Plan, and the Local Extended Care Plan. The International Standard plan covers healthcare globally, the International Premium plan includes health, dental, and vision coverages globally, with the Gold and Platinum plans including significantly higher coverage limits and additional services. The Local Standard plan covers essential health services locally, the Local Comprehensive Care Plan offers advanced specialist consultations, more extensive outpatient procedures, and broader physiotherapy and nursing care locally, and the Local Extended Care Plan provides additional specialist consultations and expanded outpatient, physiotherapy, and nursing care locally. If you would like more insight into fair equity and benefits best practices, you can download our Global Benefits Guide here !
