# Termination notice letter template

When it's necessary to part ways with an employee, a clear, respectful termination notice letter can make the process more straightforward for everyone involved. A well-structured letter provides clarity, ensures legal compliance, and sets the tone for a professional offboarding experience.
Whether you're managing a layoff, addressing performance issues, or navigating another offboarding scenario, tailor this termination notice letter template to match your internal policies, local labour laws, and the specific context.
Note : Consult local labour laws and your company’s HR policies before issuing a termination letter. Legal obligations vary by country, state, and employment type.

## Termination notice letter template

[Your name]
[Your job title]
[Company name]
[Company address]
[City, State, ZIP Code] (optional)
[Email address]
[Phone number] (optional)
[Date]
[Employee name]
[Employee job title]
[Department]
[Company name]

Subject: Notice of termination of employment
Dear [employee’s name],
This letter is to formally inform you that your employment with [company name] will be terminated effective [last working day], per [applicable contract/handbook clause or local labour law].
The reason for this decision is [insert reason — e.g., role redundancy, performance problems, policy violations]. This decision was made after [summarise process — e.g., a formal review, previous discussions, or organisational changes].
We recognise your contributions during your time here, particularly [mention any relevant positive highlights or appreciation, if appropriate].
Please find the key details regarding your termination below:

- Final working day : [Date]

- Final paycheck and benefits : You will receive your final paycheck on [date], which includes [details such as unused holiday, final pay, etc.].

- Return of company property : Please return all company equipment and access credentials (e.g., laptop, phone, ID badge) by [date].

- Continuation of benefits (if applicable): [E.g., information on insurance, health coverage, etc.]

- Reference and support (if applicable): [Include if offering support services or a reference.]

- Next steps : We will provide your final termination documents, including a separation summary and benefits information, by [date]. An exit interview will be scheduled with [HR contact] to gather your feedback and discuss the transition.

You are entitled to [mention any severance package, notice period, or accrued benefits, if applicable], as outlined in [employment contract / employee handbook].
If you have any questions about this process, your final paycheck, or other next steps, feel free to contact [representative’s name and contact info].
We appreciate the contributions you’ve made to [company] and wish you the best in your future endeavours.
Sincerely,

[Your name]
[Your title, Company]
[Signature or typed name]

## Tips for writing a professional termination notice letter

A termination letter is more than a formality; it acts as a legal document as well. Following best practices helps ensure your message is clear, compliant, and respectful, regardless of the reason for termination.

- Stay factual and objective : Focus on key details. Avoid emotional language or unnecessary elaboration.

- Clarify legal and financial information : Clearly state final pay, benefits, and what the employee is entitled to.

- Understand and follow legal requirements : Labour laws vary by country, state, and contract type. Ensure your letter meets all applicable legal standards, including notice periods, severance entitlements, and documentation protocols.

- Confirm next steps : Mention what’s needed from the employee before their departure (e.g., returning equipment, handing over work).

- Maintain respect and empathy : Even when terminations are due to misconduct or downsizing, a respectful tone helps preserve dignity and reduce potential conflict.
