## Vision

Remote offers flexible vision benefit options for employees in the United States, providing essential vision coverage through the Basic Vision plan, which is fully paid for by the company. This plan covers routine eye exams, lenses, frames, and contact lenses, ensuring employees receive necessary eye care.

For those seeking more comprehensive coverage, employees have the option to upgrade to the Premium Vision plan and pay the difference in cost. This plan offers enhanced coverage with more frequent allowances for exams, lenses, and frames, as well as higher allowances for frames and contact lenses.

Both plans offer in-network and out-of-network coverage options, ensuring that employees have access to quality vision care.

## In-Network

- Frequency:

- Exam: 12 Months

- Lenses: 12 Months

- Frames: 24 Months

- Examination: $10 Copay

- Lenses: $25 Copay (Single, Bifocal, Trifocal)

- Frames: $150 allowance + 20% discount

- Contact Lenses: $150 allowance

## Out-of-Network

- Frequency:

- Exam: 12 Months

- Lenses: 12 Months

- Frames: 24 Months

- Examination: Up to $39

- Lenses: Up to $23 (Single), $37 (Bifocal), $49 (Trifocal)

- Frames: Up to $46

- Contact Lenses: Up to $100
