## Employing in Hungary

Hungarian employment is legislated by a combination of national and international laws, matching its fellow European countries in most respects. Employers and employees in Hungary must use written employment agreements to commence employment relationships. These contracts must include several details, including the title of the position, the pay to be paid, and the location where the work will be performed. With remote work on the rise, employers must be vigilant to ensure their employment contracts in Hungary will hold up to scrutiny.

Minimum Wage

Hungary differentiates between the minimum wage and the guaranteed minimum wage. The guaranteed minimum wage only applies to full-time employees hired for jobs that require at least a secondary school qualification or secondary vocational qualification. The minimum wage applies to everyone regardless of qualifications.

The minimum wage in Hungary is 2,018.00 HUF per month and the guarantee minimum wage is 2,333.00 HUF per month, effective January 2026.

Payroll Cycle

Salaries must be paid on a monthly basis.
