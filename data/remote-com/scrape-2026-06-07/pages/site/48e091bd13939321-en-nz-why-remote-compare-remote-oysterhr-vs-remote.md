## Oyster vs Remote FAQs

How much does Remote cost versus Oyster?

Remote offers simple, transparent pricing — you’ll pay $699 per employee per month when billed annually. Contractor management is $29 per contractor. Oyster offers similar pricing, but lacks our fair price guarantee , and charges additional fees for services such as benefits administration. Oyster also uses third-party entities in many of the countries it operates in, which may incur further charges.

How many countries does Remote's EOR service cover?

Remote’s Employer of Record (EOR) service currently covers over 100+ countries worldwide, providing extensive access to global talent. And because Remote owns 100% of the entities it uses, its customers have complete oversight and control, and no risk of additional admin fees.

Why is Remote better than Oyster for global hiring?

Remote is specifically designed as a dedicated global employment partner, offering purpose-built services for payroll, EOR, and contractor management. Oyster has a solid EOR offering, but its payroll and contractor management products offer only limited features and support, meaning the platform lacks detailed functionality or flexibility for teams looking to grow globally.
