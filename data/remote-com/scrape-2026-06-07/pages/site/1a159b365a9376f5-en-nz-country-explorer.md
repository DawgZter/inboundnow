## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the legal requirements for hiring internationally?

Hiring internationally comes with complex legal requirements, from drafting locally compliant contracts and managing payroll taxes to ensuring proper worker classification and providing mandatory benefits like healthcare and paid leave.

Our Employer of Record (EOR) services simplify global hiring by acting as the legal employer in each country. We handle contracts, payroll, tax withholdings, benefits administration, and compliance, so you can onboard employees quickly without setting up a local entity. Expand your team with confidence while we take care of the legal complexities.

How to stay compliant when hiring internatonally?

Hiring abroad means opening your own legal entity, hiring payroll specialists, navigating local tax laws, and offering mandatory benefits. Companies must set up compliant payroll systems, ensure correct tax withholdings, and meet local labour requirements.

An Employer of Record (EOR) simplifies this process by acting as the legal employer. We handle payroll, tax compliance, and benefits administration, allowing you to hire internationally without setting up a local entity. Whether you’re expanding into one country or multiple, our EOR solution ensures compliance while you focus on growing your team.

What's the difference between hiring contractors vs. employees globally?

The key difference between contractors and employees comes down to control, benefits, and compliance. Contractors operate independently, managing their own schedules and taxes, while employees work under company direction and receive payroll, benefits, and legal protections.

Globally, misclassification risks can lead to legal and financial penalties. Our Employer of Record (EOR) and contractor management solutions help you hire the right workforce while ensuring compliance with local labour laws. Whether you're scaling with contractors or building a full-time team, we handle payroll, tax withholdings, and legal requirements, so you can hire with confidence anywhere in the world.
