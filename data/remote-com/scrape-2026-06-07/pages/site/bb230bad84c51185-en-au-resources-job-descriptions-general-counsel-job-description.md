# General counsel job description

At [your Company name], the General Counsel serves as the chief legal officer of the organisation, overseeing all legal aspects of the business. This role involves providing strategic legal guidance to the executive team and managing the organisation's legal affairs.

Are you a seasoned legal expert with a strategic mindset and a commitment to excellence? [Your Company name] is looking for a General Counsel to guide our legal initiatives and contribute to our overall success. Apply today to join our team and lead our legal affairs to new heights.

[Apply here] or [send your resume to careers@[yourcompany].com]

## About your company

[Your company name] is a [brief description of your company, e.g., 'leading provider of innovative technology solutions with a global reach']. We value integrity, transparency, and the rule of law as foundational elements of our business practices.

## Responsibilities

- Provide expert and strategic legal advice to management on all business-related issues.

- Oversee the work of outside counsel and coordinate legal matters handled by them.

- Ensure compliance with local, state, and federal laws and regulations applicable to the company’s operations.

- Manage the legal functions of the company, including litigation, contract negotiations, intellectual property rights, and compliance issues.

- Develop and lead corporate legal strategy to promote and protect the company's matters.

## Qualifications

- Juris Doctor (JD) degree from an accredited law school.

- Admission to practice law in the relevant jurisdiction.

- Extensive experience in practising law, preferably in a business environment or as part of a law firm.

- Proven track record of managing legal staff and working with senior management.

## Skills

- Excellent judgement and analytical skills.

- Superior negotiation and communication skills.

- Ability to work independently on complex legal matters.

- Strong leadership and organisational skills.

The General Counsel is critical in mitigating risk and guiding the company through complex legal and regulatory frameworks. Their expertise supports the company’s strategic initiatives and ensures legal compliance across all operations.

### Paths to become a General Counsel:

- Accumulate significant legal experience in corporate law, litigation, or a related field.

- Develop strong leadership capabilities and a deep understanding of business operations.

- Build a reputation for ethical practice and excellent judgement.

### Career paths for a General Counsel:

- Senior Vice President, Legal Affairs

- Chief Legal Officer

- Partner at a Law Firm

- Corporate Executive Officer

### Other possible titles for a General Counsel:

- Chief Legal Officer

- Corporate Counsel

- Senior Legal Counsel

- Vice President, Legal Affairs
