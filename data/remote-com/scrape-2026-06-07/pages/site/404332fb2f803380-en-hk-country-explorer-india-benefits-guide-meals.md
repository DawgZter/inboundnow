Meal Benefit

### Flexi Benefits

Remote offers a default monthly meal voucher benefit in India, amounting to ₹2,200 (~27 USD) per employee. This is provided through a flex benefits card, based on a standard calculation of ₹100 per day for 22 working days.

While meal vouchers are a common practice in India, they are not mandatory. This benefit is provided as a pay deduction, meaning that ₹2,200 will be deducted from the employee's gross monthly pay if they opt in for the meal vouchers. This amount is allocated to their meal vouchers. There is no additional cost to the employer. Employees enrolled under the old tax regime can benefit from tax exemptions on these vouchers, which can lead to tax savings based on their specific tax slab.
