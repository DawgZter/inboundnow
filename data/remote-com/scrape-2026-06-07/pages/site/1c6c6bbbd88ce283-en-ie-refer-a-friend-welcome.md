What is Refer a Friend?

Refer a friend is a fantastic way of rewarding customers for introducing their friends to Remote and our Employer of Record (EOR) service. We understand the power of word of mouth and want to reward our customers for spreading the word about Remote.

Why have I been sent this referral link?

You have been sent this link because your friend thinks you are a great fit for Remote and wants to help you scale your business globally. You and your friend will also receive a $300 reward for sharing the love! Make sure to book your call with our team and drop your personal code in the form when booking the call.

My friend sent me a link, how do I claim my $300 cash reward?

Please follow the steps as mentioned on this page under the. "How do you redeem your reward?" above. Then after you have gone through two successful invoice cycles with Remote (approx. 2 months). After this, we’ll send your rewards to Remote’s referral partner, Aklamio. Aklamio will contact you when the rewards have been confirmed, and you can have it paid into either your bank account or PayPal account at any time from your referral account.

Please note: If you do not complete two successful invoice cycles then unfortunately the referral will be rejected.
