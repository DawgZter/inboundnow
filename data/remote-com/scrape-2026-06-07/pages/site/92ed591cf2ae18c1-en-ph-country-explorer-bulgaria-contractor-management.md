## Contractor management in Bulgaria

Hiring contractors in Bulgaria can be a smart and efficient way for businesses to complete tasks without the commitment of full-time employment. Whether you need independent contractors for specialized services or subcontractors for other labor-intensive projects, it is important to understand Bulgaria's labor laws and regulations to ensure compliance and smooth operations.

### Understanding the legal framework in Bulgaria

Bulgaria has specific laws in place that govern contractors, and it is essential to be familiar with these to avoid legal issues. Contractors in Bulgaria are typically self-employed, which means businesses do not have to offer the same benefits and protections as employees. However, both parties must ensure that the terms of the contract are clear and comply with Bulgaria’s labor and tax regulations.
