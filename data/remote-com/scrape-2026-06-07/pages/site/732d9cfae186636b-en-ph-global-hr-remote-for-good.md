## Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign up for the program?

You can start setting up your Remote account immediately - get started here . Or if you would prefer to discuss this program further, please reach out to our team at sales@remote.com .

How long are my discounts good for?

Your discounts are effective for 12 months after signing up with Remote. Remember, we don’t require minimums to participate in the Remote for Good program. You'll enjoy 15% off our EOR and contractor management services for 12 months. Please note, the discount on our employer of record services is available for annual plans only.

What happens after the first year?

At the end of the 12-month discounted period, your account will automatically be charged the full price for each active employee and contractor in the Remote platform. No action needs to be taken on your part and your Remote experience will be uninterrupted.

Is the Remote for Good discount good on your monthly plans?

No. Only annual plans are eligible to receive the Remote for Good discount.

Are there other terms and conditions to the discount that I should be aware of?

Yes, the full terms and conditions for this discount are outlined below:

-
To be eligible, the organization must be a registered nonprofit, charity, trust, social enterprise, or benefit corporation with a mission to make a positive difference for social benefit.

-
The 15% discount on Employer of Record fees is only valid on annual, paid upfront plans.

-
The 12-month discount period begins when you agree to the Terms of Service.

-
At the end of the 12-month discount period, your account will begin to be charged the full price for each active employee in the Remote platform.

-
If you hire using this promotion and we are unable to verify your eligibility, the promotion may be removed from your account, requiring you to pay the standard price.

-
The terms and conditions of this promotion are subject to change at any time at the sole discretion of Remote.

We are an education company. Are we eligible for this program?

It depends. The program was made for any industry, but it is important that the company is a registered nonprofit, charity, social enterprise, or benefit corporation. If your education company is one of the above, then yes, you qualify.

What if I am already a Remote customer? Or past customer?

Thanks for choosing Remote! We're happy to welcome eligible current customers to the Remote for Good program during their first year of service. If you are a current customer in your first year with Remote and are interested in joining the Remote for Good program, please ask your Customer Success Manager for more details about how to get started or reach out to us at help@remote.com .

If you are a past customer, we would love to welcome you back! Past and inactive customers eligible for the Remote for Good program are encouraged to participate.

Does my location matter?

No. We are a globally distributed team and ready to help companies of all sizes and in every major region around the world.

How long are my discounts effective?

Your discounts are effective for 12 months after signing up with Remote. Remember, we don’t require minimums to participate in the Remote for Good program. You'll enjoy 20% off our EOR and contractor management services for 12 months. Please note, the discount on our employer of record services is available for annual plans only.
