## How Remote is helping displaced Ukrainian citizens

The European Union has activated the Temporary Protection Directive to grant protection for Ukrainians. 27 EU member states have agreed to grant Ukrainian citizens with residence permits for a period of 1 year, automatically extended for a further year. This directive gives Ukrainians the right to choose where they want to live, work and belong.
The Remote for Refugees program is available to help Ukrainians and anyone who has been displaced by this conflict. If you’re looking to hire Ukranian refugees remotely, Remote can provide employer of record services to help you hire and onboard refugee talent at special reduced rates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Remote offering this rate?

We have been working with governments and organisations that provide resources to refugees to come up with joint solutions that will directly help refugees rebuild their lives. This work is more urgent than ever, with millions of people being displaced from Afghanistan, Syria, and other war-torn countries. As a company that enables global employment opportunities, we feel it is our moral responsibility to help refugees get back on their feet through dignified employment.

We proudly support employers who hire refugee talent, and have made it even easier by providing a significant discount on employer on record (EOR) services. This means any refugee talent who is legally eligible to work in a country where we have an entity can be onboarded via our platform at lower cost to you. As well as supporting the employment of refugee talent, we highlight the organisations that exist to help employers locate refugee talent.

How do I sign up for the rate?

You can start by setting up your Remote account immediately - get started here ! Mention the promo code: RFR15OFF in the promo field.
When you onboard an employee, you’ll be asked to confirm your eligibility by providing documentation proving the employees’ refugee status and proper right to work or work permit(s) in their host country.
The 15% discount is valid on annual credit billing only and expires 12 months after acceptance of our terms of service.

Who qualifies for the Remote for Refugee rate?

This discount is valid for any company that uses Remote to hire refugee talent. The employer must show documentation proving the employees’ refugee status and proper right to work or work permit(s) in their host country, which must be different from their home country or previous country of residence.

Does the Remote for Refugee rate expire?

The Remote for Refugees promotion will remain open until December 31, 2026 for companies and employees that qualify; at that time, Remote reserves the right to end, change, or extend this promotion with the same or new terms.
The 15% discount is valid for all employees hired by an eligible company via Remote’s EOR service for a period of 12 months from acceptance of our terms of service.

What host countries can this rate be activated?

As long as the refugee has the legal right-to-work (i.e. work permit), we can employ them in any country where we have an entity. Check our country explorer for our live countries. If you are a refugee and you’re unsure if you have the right-to-work, we strongly recommend discussing your situation with your resettlement representative or local authority.

Can I combine this rate with other Remote offers?

No, this offer cannot be combined with other Remote offers.

I’m a refugee, how do I activate this rate?

The Remote for Refugee rate is for employers who are wanting to hire refugee talent. If you are a refugee, we highly encourage applying for roles through refugee-specific job boards or discussing your candidacy with various refugee recruitment agencies. If you’re in conversation with an employer, you can also let them know about this offer, or add a note in your CV. To reiterate, you will need to have the right to work or work permit in your host country to be hired via Remote.

I work for a refugee support organisation and want to learn more.

Please email our team at growth@remote.com to discuss how Remote can help you and the refugee talent your organisation represents. Alternatively, two talent finding resources are offering special offers:

- FlexJobs is offering a special offer for your remote-work seeking refugees to access their service; please contact Julie Mahoney at julie@flexjobs.com and mention this initiative. FlexJobs offers career coaching, resume assistance, and access to world-class employers seeking remote talent.

- Jobs for Humanity is offering free posting of jobs for refugee talent and for refugees to apply for a limited time.

I work for a government agency and want to learn more.

Please email our team at growth@remote.com and partnerships@remote.com to discuss how Remote and your agency could work closely to boost dignified employment for your refugee talent.
