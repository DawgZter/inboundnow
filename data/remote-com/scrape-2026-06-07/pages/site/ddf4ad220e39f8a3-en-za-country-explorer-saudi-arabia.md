## Employing in Saudi Arabia

Provisions for employee protections and workers’ rights are spelled out in the Saudi Arabian Labour Law of 2006 and are enforced jointly by the Ministry of Labour and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development.
Employees in Saudi Arabia enjoy protection against discrimination based on age, religion, and race.
Common questions that could come up during the hiring process include the minimum wage, overtime rates, and guaranteed paid time off. Remote can help you offer a complete, competitive, and compliant benefits package to your employees in Saudi Arabia.

Minimum wage

Saudi citizens have a minimum wage set at 4,000 SAR per month. There is no legally specified minimum wage for foreign workers; the market typically determines their wages or set by the terms of their individual employment contracts.

Payroll cycle

Wages can either be paid on a weekly or monthly basis.
