# Talent Management

Simplify how you manage your global talent across the employee lifecycle, from onboarding to offboarding.

## Global talent management made easy

Cross tedious employee management tasks off your to-do list with the Remote Global HR platform.

Get rid of guesswork

Not sure how much to offer candidates in new countries or for new roles? Get to 'yes' sooner with compensation insights in Remote.
With access to our proprietary pay benchmarking data, you can make job offers with confidence, knowing they're competitive and fair in each candidate's local market.

Track employee changes

- Send and track employee probation letters

- Easily follow employee transitions through probation, promotion, pay changes, and more

- Access detailed employment history records at any time

- Get a bird's-eye view of team updates, upcoming public holidays, to-dos and more in your dashboard

- Ensure compliance with embedded country and situation-specific guidance for employees hired via Remote Employer of Record

Manage documents and requests

- Store critical employee records like contracts, equity agreements, remote work requests, and othe documents

- Both employers and employees can instantly access documents and requests, see the status, and change visibility for sensitive information

Offboard employees

- Track resignations and terminations in Remote, archive past employee profiles, and give employees access to their payslip history

- Get country-specific insights to help you navigate compliance, with the integrated Offboarding Assistant

- Ensure lawful employee offboarding in accordance with contract terms and local regulations for employees hired via Remote Employer of Record

- EOR employee administration is handled by Remote, managing notice periods, severance payments, and other offboarding tasks too

Expedite international hiring and onboarding

## You find the candidate, we help with the rest

Reduce the time to hire with automated processes, including guided new hire workflows and self-onboarding data collection from your new team member.
For EOR employees, we manage the entire onboarding journey on your behalf: generating localised contracts, and ensuring full compliance every step of the way.
With onboarding managed, you can shift your focus back to finding the world's best talent, and welcoming your new starters.
