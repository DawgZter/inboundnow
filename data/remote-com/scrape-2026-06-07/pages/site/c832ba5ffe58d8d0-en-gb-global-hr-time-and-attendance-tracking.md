## FAQ

What is time and attendance software?

In human resources, time and attendance software is used to track and manage when employees and contractors work. Many organisations use it to ensure proper coverage and productivity. The ability to track precise working hours is crucial for users who are paid according to the amount of time worked. Many countries also have time and attendance requirements that must be met for the organisation to remain legally compliant.

What are the benefits of automated time and attendance records?

Organisations need to track time and attendance to ensure legal compliance and payroll accuracy. Automating time and attendance records can reduce effort and increase quality. Remote has built smart automation into our time and attendance software. Examples include the ability to clock in and out, the ability to set timesheets for automatic submission, and automated approval flows for time off requests.

Who can use Remote to track time and attendance?

Any employee or contractor who has been onboarded to Remote can track their working hours, submit timesheets, view available time off, and request time off from the web platform or mobile app.
