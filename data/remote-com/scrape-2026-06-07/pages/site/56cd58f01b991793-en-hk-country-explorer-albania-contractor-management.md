## Contractor management in Albania

Hiring contractors in Albania can be a strategic and cost-effective way for businesses to complete projects without the obligations of hiring full-time employees. Whether you are engaging independent contractors for specialized services or working with subcontractors for various projects, understanding Albania’s labor laws and regulations is essential.

Albania has specific rules regarding contractor classification, taxation, and employment rights. Businesses must ensure they correctly classify workers to avoid potential misclassification risks, which can lead to fines or legal challenges. Contractors in Albania typically operate under a business-to-business agreement and are responsible for their own taxes and social contributions. However, it is crucial to have clear, legally compliant contracts to protect both parties.
