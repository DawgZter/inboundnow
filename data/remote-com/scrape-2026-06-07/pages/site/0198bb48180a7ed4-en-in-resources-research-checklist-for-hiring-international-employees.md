Building a global team doesn’t have to be complicated. But you do need to make sure your company is ready to manage a distributed team, and even more importantly – your employment agreements must comply with all relevant labour laws. Use this checklist to guide your process and keep your business protected.
Work through this checklist before you hire an international employee. The steps outlined will help you spot potential problem areas before you commit to an offer.
