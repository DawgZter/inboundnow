# What is a work permit?

## What is a work permit?

A "work permit" is a legal document that officially allows a non-citizen to work in a foreign country. In essence, it's a stamp of approval from the governing body of a country, allowing an individual to earn a living within its borders.
With globalisation leading to increased workforce mobility and globally distributed teams , businesses often need to hire employees from different countries. But navigating the maze of international employment laws is no easy task. Each country has its own regulations regarding foreign workers, and these rules can change unexpectedly. Ensuring every employee has the correct right-to-work permissions is a critical aspect of maintaining a compliant, effective, and diverse workforce.

## Why must HR leaders ensure compliance with laws around work permits?

There are several reasons why HR leaders must understand the laws around work permits and ensure proper compliance.
To comply with local labour laws
Noncompliance with international employment legislation can lead to substantial penalties, tarnish a company's reputation, and even result in legal action. For instance, in the United States, employers who violate immigration laws may face fines ranging from $375 to $16,000 per violation, depending on the nature and number of offences. In the UK, the penalty can be as high as £20,000 for each illegally employed worker. Clearly, the financial risks involved with noncompliance are significant.
To promote fair employment practices
Ensuring the correct right-to-work permissions safeguards against exploitation and ensures individuals are rightfully entitled to the same protections and benefits as local employees. This way, businesses can create a respectful and equitable work environment, fostering stronger employee engagement and loyalty.
To ensure business continuity
Lastly, it ensures business continuity. If a company is found to be non-compliant, they might be required to terminate the employment of workers who do not have the correct permits, which can disrupt operations and impact productivity.
