## Retirement & Pension

Remote offers a mandatory Provident Fund in South Africa as part of its Basic Benefits package to ensure compliance with local retirement savings practices. This Provident Fund applies to all employees.

-

### Contribution Overview:

- Employer Contribution: 5% of base pay.

- Employee Contribution: 5% of base pay, with the option to increase the contribution up to 27.5% voluntarily.

- The contribution rates are fixed, with no flexibility for employer contributions beyond 5%.

### Key Features:

- Provident Fund: The fund serves as a retirement savings plan for employees, providing long-term financial security.

- No Opt-Out: Employees cannot opt out of this mandatory benefit.

- Waiver of Premium: In the event of long-term illness or disability, premiums are waived to ensure continuous pension accumulation.

- Pension Transfer: Employees can transfer their existing funds to another pension provider upon leaving Remote.

### Additional Benefits:

- Disability Coverage: The plan includes disability coverage, ensuring income protection if an employee is unable to work due to illness or injury.

- Survivor’s Pension: Ensures financial security for the employee’s family in case of death.
