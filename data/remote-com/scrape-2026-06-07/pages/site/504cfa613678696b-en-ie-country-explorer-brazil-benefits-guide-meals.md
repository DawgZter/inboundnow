## Meals

In Brazil, employers are required to provide monthly meal vouchers to employees. Remote ensures compliance by paying the meal voucher amount directly into the employee’s Flash cards by the 30th of each month.

When onboarding a new hire, Remote offers the Standard, Premium, and Prestige options of meal vouchers, which offer different amounts. It is important to note that what is offered to employees in Brazil will apply to all existing and future employees hired in the country.

-

Standard Voucher

Provides a monthly meal voucher amount of BRL 660 (~USD 133), which equates to a daily rate of R$30 (~USD 6) for meal expenses.

-

Premium Voucher

Provides a monthly meal voucher amount of BRL 880 (~USD 178), which equates to a daily rate of R$40 (~USD 8) for meal expenses.

-

Prestige Voucher

Provides a monthly meal voucher amount of BRL 1000 (~USD 200), which equates to a daily rate of R$45.45 (~USD 10) for meal expenses.
