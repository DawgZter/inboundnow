## What can I talk to an expert about?

Ask an Expert covers a wide range of employment questions. Here are just a few examples of what you can book calls about.

Time and Attendance

Understand how statutory sick leave, family leave, and annual leave policies apply in your target country, so you can manage leave types and support employees compliantly.

Complex terminations

Plan compliant exits with clarity on notice periods, just cause, probation rules, and dismissal risks, all tailored to local requirements.

Tax

Talk through employment-related tax questions, including gross-ups, PE risk, CIT/PIT, and total cost of employment across different countries and contexts. Available exclusively to EOR customers.

Mobility

Navigate immigration, relocation, and cross-border work rules. Whether you’re hiring a foreign national or managing business travel, we’ll help you navigate compliance.

OHS and Workers Compensation

Understand your responsibilities around workplace safety policies and insurance requirements; available in select countries.

Hiring challenges

Get guidance on sourcing, interviewing, and employer branding across markets, so you can improve time-to-hire and attract the right talent globally.

Benefits

Understand which statutory and optional benefits apply in your target country, so you can offer competitive, compliant packages without overextending your budget.
