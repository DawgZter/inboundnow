## Retirement & Pension

Remote offers a versatile occupational pension scheme (bAV) for employees in Germany. This modern solution simplifies pension management, offering flexibility and comprehensive financial protection.

-

### Contribution Overview:

- Employee Contributions: Employees can contribute between 1% and 4% of gross salary or choose a fixed amount up to €302 per month (2024 limit).

- Employer Contributions: Employers provide a 15% subsidy on employee contributions, with optional matching models or fixed contributions ranging from 1-8% of salary, depending on the employer’s preferences.

### Key Features:

- Effortless Digital Management: Through an online platform, employees can easily tailor their contributions, view contracts, and track their pension growth. The platform offers a clear, user-friendly interface to manage everything in real-time.

- Transferability: Employees can bring existing pension savings into the new plan, or choose to keep them in separate funds.

- Eligibility: This plan is available to all employees upon request, providing flexibility and additional financial security.
