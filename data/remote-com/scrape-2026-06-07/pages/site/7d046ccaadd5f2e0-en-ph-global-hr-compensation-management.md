## Frequently Asked Questions

When will Remote Compensation for compensation management be available?

We’re targeting Q4 2025. Join the waitlist for updates and early access.

Is this available as part of Remote HRIS or a separate add-on?

Remote Compensation is an add-on to Remote HRIS. It’s deeply integrated: no third-party tools needed.

Can I define and enforce salary bands across countries?

Yes. You can create bands by role, level, and location, and Remote will surface alignment for every employee.

Will managers be able to suggest compensation changes directly?

Yes. Remote Compensation enables manager self-serve with role-based access, budget rules, and approval flows.

Does this connect with Remote Payroll?

Yes. Once comp changes are approved, they automatically sync to employee profiles and payroll - no manual updates needed.
