## Frequently Asked Questions

What are the legal requirements for hiring across multiple states?

Hiring across multiple states means navigating state-specific tax laws, payroll requirements, and labour regulations. Employers must register in each state where employees work, withhold the correct state taxes, comply with wage laws, and provide mandatory benefits like workers' compensation and unemployment insurance. Some states also have unique requirements for paid leave, overtime, and employer-sponsored benefits.

Managing compliance across multiple states can be complex. Our Employer of Record (EOR) solution simplifies multi-state hiring by handling payroll, tax withholdings, and compliance, so you can expand your workforce anywhere in the U.S. without the administrative burden.

How do companies stay compliant when hiring across the U.S.?

Each U.S. state has unique payroll taxes, labour laws, and mandatory benefits. Employers must manage tax withholdings, social security contributions, and compliance with state-specific regulations.

Our Employer of Record (EOR) solution streamlines multi-state hiring by handling payroll, tax filings, and compliance, so you can onboard employees anywhere in the U.S. without setting up separate state registrations. Whether hiring in one state or across many, we ensure compliance while you focus on running your business.

What's the difference between hiring contractors vs. employees in the U.S.?

In the U.S., contractors are self-employed workers who provide services to businesses on a project or freelance basis, while employees are hired directly by a company and receive wages, benefits, and workplace protections under federal and state laws. Employers must also comply with tax withholding and labour regulations for employees, whereas contractors manage their own taxes and benefits.

Misclassifying workers can lead to IRS penalties, back taxes, and compliance risks. Our Employer of Record (EOR) and contractor management solutions help businesses navigate state-specific labour laws, ensuring proper classification, payroll management, and tax compliance, so you can hire with confidence across all 50 states.

How do you choose the best U.S. state to hire for my specific needs?

The best state for remote hiring depends on labour laws, payroll taxes, and industry-specific talent pools. Compare states based on pay expectations, tax rates, and ease of compliance. Our Employer of Record (EOR) simplifies hiring across multiple states by managing payroll, tax withholdings, and compliance, so you can scale your workforce without the administrative burden.
