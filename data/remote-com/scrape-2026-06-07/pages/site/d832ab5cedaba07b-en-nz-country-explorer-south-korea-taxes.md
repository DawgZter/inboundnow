## Taxes in South Korea

Learn how employment taxes affect your payroll and your employees’ paychecks in South Korea.

Employer

1.15%

Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance is calculated at 4.50% of the base monthly pay which is capped at 5,900,000.

4%

National Health Insurance Contribution is calculated at 4% of the base monthly pay (NHI: 3.54% + LTCI 0.46%). A monthly cap of 3,653,550 applies.

1.15%

Employment insurance is calculated at 1.15% of the base monthly pay.

0.4590%

Long-term care Insurance is calculated at 0.4590% (12.95% of the NHI contribution rate) of the base monthly pay. A monthly cap of 3,653,550 applies.
