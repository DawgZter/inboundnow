## Life Insurance

Remote provides a robust life insurance and AD&D program for all full-time U.S. employees. The Basic Life Insurance plan delivers a lump-sum payment to your designated beneficiary in the event of your passing, covering both natural causes, illnesses and accidents. In tandem, the Basic Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) policy offers additional protection in the case of an accident.

-

Coverages

## Life Insurance

- Coverage Amounts:

- Covers 2x Annual Pay up to $400,000

- Provides a lump-sum payment to your designated beneficiary upon your passing.

- Covers all causes of death, including natural causes, illnesses, and accidents.

## Accidental Death and Dismemberment (AD&D) Insurance

- Coverage Amounts:

- Same as Life Insurance coverage amounts.

- Provides additional financial protection specifically for accidents.

- Offers a benefit for accidental death or certain injuries resulting in dismemberment.
